Where my two favourite things meet: chess and code.

AI Chess Board

A system that uses computer vision to "see" a physical or digital chessboard, work out the position, and pass it to a chess engine like Stockfish for live analysis, move hints, or automated play.

Hardware

  • Overhead camera: a USB webcam, Raspberry Pi camera, or phone camera placed directly above the board.
  • Processor: a laptop or PC, or a single-board computer such as a Raspberry Pi 4/5 or NVIDIA Jetson Nano.
  • Chess set: high-contrast pieces track best.
